About & data sources

The land outline and prefecture borders come from 地球地図日本 (国土地理院 / GSI, via dataofjapan/land). Stations, line order and colours outside the hand-made Tokyo/Shinkansen data come from open data: station_database (駅データ.jp, CC BY 4.0) and MLIT 国土数値情報 N02; optional real timetables from ODPT. Train movements are simulated (not official timetables), English names are machine-romanised, and track shapes come from MLIT section geometry (a few edges fall back to straight lines).
